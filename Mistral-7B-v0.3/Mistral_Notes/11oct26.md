### an epoch figure for later

The progress bar reads `Epoch 0.01/12` over 1000 steps, giving ~88 optimiser steps per epoch (1409 blocks / 16 = 88.1, and ceil(1000/88) = 12). Against an evaluation cadence of 50 that is 0.57 epochs per evaluation.

This places Mistral between the two models where the question has been settled. Qwen samples at 0.25 epochs per evaluation and its post-floor overfitting resolved into a six-tread staircase with every riser on an epoch boundary; 
Phi samples at 2.5 and the same increments aliased into a smooth climb. Mistral sits just under one, so if this phase ever reaches an overfitting regime the structure should be marginally resolvable. P3 is a 1000-step refinement 
rather than a fit-to-convergence, so it may not get there, but the figure is worth having on record.
