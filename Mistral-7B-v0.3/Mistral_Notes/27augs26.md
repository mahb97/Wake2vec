# wake2vec devlog 2026-08-26

> *P3 launch*

## Mistral 7B v0.3 P3 LAUNCH 

P2 closed at 1200 with a clean overfit arc and best val 4.705 at step 600.

the null has now held across four configurations (TinyLlama P3 and P3b, Llama 1B P3, Llama 3B P3): L_morph immovable at the fourth decimal, L_device a random walk in the 0.17 to 0.24 band, Wake isotropy pinned at 0.998, and under 
λ_morph=50 the 3B's Wake rows drifting only cosine 0.9998 across 600 steps. near-perfect isotropy leaves nothing for the auxiliary losses to amplify. on a near-uniform sphere there is no preferential direction for device clusters to form in, 
and the triplet loss is asking embeddings to organise by word-formation process when they encode meaning and usage.

for Mistral, its Wake region came out of P1 at isotropy 0.995, the only one in the lineup below the 0.998 attractor, on the largest embedding reorganisation in the set (drift cosine 0.485), with 
full neologisms rather than truncated boundary tokens as the most-displaced rows. if any configuration has deposited internal structure for strong lambdas to grab, it is this one.

- non-null (Wake isotropy moves measurably, direction consistency rises, L_morph descends): the first geometric movement in the project, and it would localise the null's cause precisely, at insufficient reorganisation rather than at a
structural incompatibility between the objective and the geometry. the reading would become: the losses need structure to amplify, and P1 has to supply it.
- null again (Wake rows hold at cosine ~0.999, L_morph pinned, L_device wandering its band): the strongest available statement of the finding, because it comes from the one configuration that had the geometry to break it.
