If it lands, the descent reads 0.6700, 0.6672, 0.6545, 0.6370 with deltas of -0.0028, -0.0127, -0.0175. Monotonic and accelerating across all three intervals, and excess over chance up 72% from the baseline in 150 steps, against a quantity that under the 
previous formulation did not move at the fourth decimal across 2,000 steps and 500x lambda amplification. A loss that falls faster the longer it runs is finding a direction. Whether that continues past 150 is the first genuinely new information this phase 
will produce, since everything to 150 is now replay.

### LM Side 

Validation LM sits at 4.4308 against P2's minimum of 4.705, so P3 is improving held-out language modelling by roughly 0.3 rather than degrading it, which unfreezing 44,553 embedding rows would predict. The direction between the two evaluations is upward, but 
from a position better than the phase it inherited.

If val LM continues to rise while morph continues to fall, the auxiliary objective is buying geometric structure at a measurable cost in fit, and the size of that cost becomes a reportable quantity. If val LM turns back down, the +0.0501 was the transient of 
unfreezing rather than a trend.

### pre-registered outcomes

- Movement continues. L_morph descends past 0.6370 and excess over chance rises past +0.0787. The informative form is per-group: if the groups that gain are the 15 of 130 that already cleared z = -3 at baseline, the objective amplifies existing structure.

Everything after that seam describes a fresh embedding under a schedule that believed it was at step 100, so the descent it recorded, excess over chance rising +0.0459 to +0.0920 across 100 steps, is a real observation of warm moments applied to cold weights rather than a continuation of anything.
