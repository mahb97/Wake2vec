# wake2vec devlog 2026-08-30

> *P3 relaunches*

## Mistral 7B v0.3 P3 RELAUNCH from step 0 

the first P3 launch on the 28th was stopped at ~step 100. `L_morpheme` was computing an unnormalised squared Euclidean deviation of direction vectors from their group mean, with no cosine anywhere, so its value scaled as 
(embedding magnitude)². on Mistral's embedding scale (base row norm 0.17 over 4096 dims) that evaluated to ~1e-6 and printed as `0.0000` at every step. λ_morph=50 therefore contributed about 5e-5 against an LM loss of 4.5, 
roughly 0.001% of the objective.

the loss is now `mean(1 − cos(direction_i, group_mean))`, which is the exact quantity `analyze_morpheme_group` already reported, so for the first time the optimised metric and the reported metric are the same thing. 
λ recalibrated 50.0 to 1.0: under cosine the loss is O(1), so 50 would have been ~10x the LM term and destroyed the model, while 1.0 puts the morpheme term at roughly 15-20% of the objective, comparable to the 12% share 
L_device carried at P3b. 

the Llama 3B's post-hoc analysis found direction consistency of 0.10 to 0.24 on well-sampled groups, which under this loss corresponds to an entry value of roughly 0.76 to 0.90. so:

- step-0 L_morph below ~0.76: Mistral's Wake region carries more compositional structure than the 3B's did, the isotropy-0.995 premise is vindicated, and the null-breaker has a live shot.
- step-0 L_morph at 0.85 to 0.90: the Wake region is no more compositionally structured than anything else in the lineup, and the reorganisation that dropped it below the 0.998 attractor
did not deposit morpheme structure specifically.

---

both deltas now compute on one code path inside the script: pre-versus-post isotropy per population, and direction consistency paired by morpheme, so every group is compared against its own entry value rather than against a lineup 
average.

half the morpheme signal is ordinary English inflection, and some of it is malformed. 50.7% of the 5,924 pairs sit in ten groups (`-s`, `-ed`, `-y`, `-ing`, `-er`, `-ly`, `-on`, `-'s`, `co-`, `-a`), and several of those 
segmentations are orthographic strips rather than morpheme boundaries: `Action→Acti`, `Concern→ncern`, `Africa→Afric`, `after→aft`. where the base is a non-word, the displacement vector has no reason to align with anything 
and the loss cannot be satisfied on that group at any lambda.
