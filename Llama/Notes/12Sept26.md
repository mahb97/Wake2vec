## Llama 3.1-8B P3 session 1 

The run is live and past the blocker that held it since the 6th. Three optimisers were tried before one fitted: Adafactor holds three full-shape fp32 buffers to update a bf16 parameter, 7.89GB of transient for a 706M-parameter embedding; 
paged 8-bit AdamW moved that demand into host memory and terminated the runtime; and `TrainingArguments(optim="adamw_bnb_8bit")` silently resolved to bitsandbytes' 32-bit AdamW, whose fp32 moments came to 5.26GB. Constructing `AdamW8bit` 
directly brings optimiser state to 1.32GB and the run fits.

0.083. On the standing structural diagnosis that geometry offers an auxiliary objective nothing to amplify. It amplified anyway. The consequence is the one pre-registered for this branch: the geometric instruments, not the geometry, were the 
limiting factor. Isotropy, pairwise cosine, nearest-neighbour structure and the eigenspectrum all describe this region as featureless, and a correctly scaled objective moves it regardless. The four earlier P3 nulls were obtained under a defective

The isotropy ordering holds on both readings. Mistral entered below the attractor at 0.995 and carries the larger baseline effect (+0.0458 against +0.0362, so this model at 79%) and now also the faster response (34% against 15% relative gain). 
Isotropy tracks both how much compositional structure is present and how much of it is available to gradient. The informative form remains per-group: whether the 24 groups that cleared z = -3 at baseline are the ones gaining.

### the language-modelling side runs opposite to Mistral's

The auxiliary offset at step 100 is 1.0774, giving val LM 5.1701 against this model's P2 best of 5.1486. P3 is therefore costing roughly 0.02 in held-out language modelling where Mistral's P3 was improving on its P2 by 0.27. The train-validation 
gap of +0.80 is consistent with that reading.

This is the only run in the lineup with quantised optimiser state; TinyLlama, Llama 1B, Llama 3B and Mistral all use fp32 `adamw_torch`. Transformers registers embedding layers for 32-bit state under bitsandbytes by default, a stability measure, 
and constructing the optimiser directly bypasses it. 8-bit blockwise error obscures movement rather than manufacturing it, so a measured descent is conservative.

Step time is 237s, giving roughly 66 hours for 1000 steps. At four-hour sessions that is fifteen to seventeen resumes, and `EmbeddingRestore` has not yet executed: the smoke test's second pass was skipped. The first cut is therefore also 
the first test of the resume path.
