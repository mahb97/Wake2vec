Adafactor replaces AdamW because the embedding matrix is 172,451 x 4096 = 706M parameters and the gradient mask zeroes gradients after they are materialised, so the optimizer carries state for the whole matrix: approximately 5.65GB of fp32 moments, 
which does not fit a T4 alongside a 4-bit 8B body. The earlier P3 runs used AdamW and their device-side results are sound, so this weakens a comparison that exists; it is recorded as a limitation rather than treated as costless.

### the prior

This model is the control, not the null-breaker. Its P2 embedding analysis places the Wake region at 0.998453 isotropy, at the attractor observed across the lineup and above the base vocabulary's own 0.9896, with base-new pairwise cosine 0.0009 and no 
nearest neighbour above 0.083. On the standing structural diagnosis, that near-uniform geometry offers the auxiliary losses nothing to amplify, and the prior on movement is low.

Mistral has now supplied the positive case. Its P3 recorded L_morpheme descending 0.6700, 0.6672, 0.6545 across steps 0, 50 and 100, with excess over a permutation chance floor rising from +0.0457 to +0.0612, a 34% gain in effect size, against a loss 
that under the previous formulation did not move at the fourth decimal across 2,000 steps. L_device showed no trend over the same interval.

So the question here is if a correctly scaled morpheme objective also move a region that entered at the attractor, or is movement confined to the one model that entered below it at 0.995? Mistral is the positive case; this is the control; the pair 
constitutes the experiment. Two further considerations support running it regardless of the prior. It is the first P3 on a 128K-vocabulary model under an objective at a scale that can affect the embeddings, the four earlier runs having tested the 
device objective and left the morpheme objective untested. And it carries the set's only compositional 1.0x initialisation, varying initialisation against every other P3.

The P2 battery returned roughly 70% verified Wake-lexicon retrieval, 42 confirmed tokens against 16 inventions, predominantly corpus hapaxes, placed in grammatical position and recurring across independent samples. Those retrievals come from a region 
that isotropy, pairwise cosine, nearest-neighbour structure and eigenspectrum all describe as featureless. A null here would therefore establish that the geometric instruments do not detect the property responsible, rather than that the property is 
absent. 

### pre-registered outcomes

- Movement: L_morpheme descends and excess over chance increases. This would be the first geometric movement recorded from a region measured at the attractor, and would indicate that the instruments rather than the geometry are the limiting factor.
- Null: Both unchanged. A null obtained under a correctly scaled objective on audited supervision against a measured chance floor, and materially different in evidential status from the four that preceded it.

The informative form in either case is per-group: if the groups that gain are those that already carried structure, the objective amplifies rather than creates, which holds independently of the aggregate.

### baseline 

The prior is quantified, and it points the way the isotropy story predicts. This model enters P3 with 79% of Mistral's effect size (+0.0362 against +0.0458). Mistral entered below the isotropy attractor at 0.995 and carries more measurable compositional structure; this model entered at the attractor, 0.998453, and carries less. That is the first quantitative confirmation that the isotropy reading tracks something real about compositional structure rather than being an incidental observation, and it arrives before training begins.

But the structure is distributed differently, and this was not predicted. Twenty-four of 118 groups clear z = -3 here, holding 36.2% of pairs, against Mistral's 15 of 130 holding 15.2%. So the 8B has a smaller total effect spread across more of its supervision, while Mistral has a larger effect concentrated in fewer groups. On Mistral, the informative test is whether the Latinate nominalisation cluster moves while the chance-level groups do not, because the structure is concentrated there. Here more than a third of the supervision sits in significant groups, so a per-group comparison has more units to work with, and a diffuse gain would be legible in a way it would not be on Mistral.

The strongest groups differ almost entirely. Mistral's are `-ion` (-18.4), `-ation` (-16.7), `-fication`, `-ition`, `-ure`, `-ive`, a single Latinate nominalisation family. This model's are `un-` (-11.7), `-fication` (-10.9), `ex-` (-10.3), `suc-` (-8.9), `-ive` (-7.7), `dif-` (-7.0), a mixture of negation, Latinate prefixes and suffixes. Only `-fication` and `-ive` appear in both.
