**Best val 8.346755 at `checkpoint-800`, final.**

Four consecutive rises, training monotone across all six intervals, and a gap that has widened at every single one from +0.182 to +0.586. Phi remains the only model in the lineup to satisfy the divergence criterion without qualification. The loss curve 
has said everything it can. P2's LoRA trains on Finnegans Wake text, so validation can fall on register, punctuation density and common-token distribution with no improvement whatsoever at Wake-neologism positions. The descent of 2.887 through the P1 
wall is a fact about fit, not about Wake.

Further steps can add a fifth and sixth rising reading. They cannot change the best checkpoint, which has been fixed since step 800, and they cannot sharpen a criterion already met cleanly. 

**The verdict is the nv-recall memorisation-versus-transformation test at `checkpoint-800`**, and Qwen has now set the standard for how it should be reported: word-level types against the full training corpus, plus the longest matching span. Qwen 
returned no memorisation signal at all, its longest match across five samples being five high-frequency function words against 219,481 training tokens.

Phi is the hard case and the reason the 2x2 exists. Its P1 produced base-vocabulary noise without a single Wake token, the least favourable prior in the set, and it floors **3.64 above Mistral's 4.705** on a configuration matched in vocabulary, Wake 
share, initialisation, family, cadence and hyperparameters, differing only in pretraining data. So the question the battery answers is not whether Phi learned something. It is whether filtered textbook pretraining permits the Wake register at all under 
injection and routing, or whether it permits only the fit.

**If Phi produces Wake register**, H1 holds even where the pretraining data works against it, and the 2x2 reports a pretraining-data effect on the achievable floor with no effect on the attainable style. That is the stronger and more surprising result.

**If Phi does not**, H1 acquires a boundary condition: embedding injection plus routing recovers the Wake register on web-pretrained models and not on filtered-corpus ones. Narrower, still a finding, and the only one in the lineup with a controlled 
comparison behind it.
