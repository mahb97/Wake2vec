# wake2vec devlog 2026-08-11

> *Phi enters P2*

## Phi-3.5-mini-instruct P2 launch

Phi starts P2 today, the phase that decides its half of the 2x2. P1 closed as the cleanest data wall in the lineup: memorised train, walled val (~12.28, worse than baseline), Wake embeddings frozen near spherical init 
(drift 0.9685, the least in the set), and a P1 generation of pure base-vocab noise (no Wake at all). P2 loads those near-baseline embeddings, freezes them, and applies LoRA to the attention and MLP projections. 
the question is whether routing can extract anything from embeddings that never learned Wake, or whether the wall extends into P2.

for P2 the source is P1 step 300 (best val, the least-overfit embeddings), with LoRA r=8 on Phi's fused targets (qkv_proj, gate_up_proj, down_proj), LR 2e-5, SEQ_LEN 512, 3000 steps, eval every 100. first eval at step 100.

### P2 loss table

| Step | Train | Val | Note |
|------|-------|-----|------|
| 100 | 11.252 | 11.234 | descended ~1.0 off the P1 wall, and crossed below the ~11.25 uniform baseline P1 never beat in 3000 steps. the data wall did NOT extend into P2 at the LM level. |

for context, the Mistral, 8B and Qwen P2s descended hard off their P1 minima because their embeddings were richly learned (Mistral P2 fell from 10.92 into the 4s; the 8B crossed the 3B's wall). Phi's embeddings are near-baseline, so:

- val descends meaningfully (say below ~11, toward the other P2s' range): LoRA routing is doing the work the embeddings could not, extracting usable signal from a near-random Wake subspace. the wall was an embedding-level phenomenon that routing partly overcomes.
- val walls again (holds near ~12.2, or barely moves): the data wall extends into P2, textbook pretraining resists Wake at both the embedding and the routing level. this would be the strong 2x2 result.

either way the LM loss is only the preview. main Q: does routed Phi transform* the Wake or regurgitate memorised training blocks. given a P1 this empty, the prior is the least favourable in the lineup, which is what makes 
it the sharp end of the Phi-vs-Mistral comparison.
