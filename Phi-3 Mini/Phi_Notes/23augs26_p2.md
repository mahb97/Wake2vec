the P2 best-vals in the set sit in a band (Mistral 4.705, the 8B 5.1486, the 3B's wall 5.33, Qwen 5.9209), and it is tempting to ask where Phi lands in it but most of that band is off-limits. the 8B and 3B are 128K-vocab and Qwen is 152K, 
so their validation token mixtures differ from Phi's and the absolute values are not comparable.

Mistral is 32K-vocab, 58% Wake share, spherical 1.5x init, same family, differing only in pretraining data, so Phi's number and Mistral's number are directly comparable. that makes Mistral's 4.705 the single reference point that means anything for Phi, 
and it converts the 2x2 from a qualitative story into one distance.

For the upcoming steps:

- Phi descends into Mistral's neighbourhood (floors somewhere in the 5s or below): the data wall is a P1-only effect. textbook pretraining prevents the embeddings from reorganising, but routing recovers what the embeddings could not learn, and by the end of P2 the two halves of the 2x2 are close. the training-data effect would then be a *phase-localised* result, real but survivable, and the 2x2 verdict would rest entirely on generation.
- Phi floors well above it (stalls around 7 or 8 while Mistral sat at 4.705): the wall did not disappear, it moved. not a wall against descent but a higher floor, the textbook prior costing a fixed amount of achievable fit that no amount of routing buys back. that is the stronger and more publishable version of the finding, because it survives into the phase that was supposed to rescue it.
