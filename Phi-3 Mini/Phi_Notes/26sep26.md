Phi is the model where the reading rules actually work. The divergence criterion needs a monotone training curve, which Qwen cannot supply and which is why its turn is permanently unresolvable. Phi supplies one: 8.165028, 8.094781, 8.033964, 
7.983980, falling at every interval with decrements of -0.070, -0.061, -0.050 that are themselves smoothly contracting. Against that, the gap has widened without a reversal: +0.182, +0.253, +0.313, +0.380. So a second rise is not ambiguous here in 
the way it would be elsewhere in the lineup.

If validation rises again, Phi has a confirmed turn, and the lineup needs a morphology it does not currently have: a turn arriving after a 200-step rest at the floor, distinct from the gentle-monotonic turn and the soft-floor turn because the flat 
stretch preceded it rather than followed it. Step 800 stays the best checkpoint. If validation falls back toward 8.347, the 0.0176 was a wobble and the asymptote reading returns. 

The run may also end itself. Validation rose at 900, so the early-stopping counter is no longer at zero, and a second rise moves it closer to terminating. That is an acceptable ending: the phase has what it needs from the loss curve either way.

## misc 

The floor is real and the 2x2 rests on its height. Three evaluations within 0.000693 across 200 steps is not undone by what follows them. Against Mistral, matched in vocabulary, Wake share, initialisation, family, cadence and hyperparameters and 
differing only in pretraining data, Phi floors 3.64 above the 4.705 minimum. Both models improved substantially under routing, so the pretraining-data effect appears as a higher achievable floor rather than as a failure to learn.

The two phases must not be conflated. In P1 validation never descended: a shallow minimum at step 300 (12.233), then a rise to 12.288 by step 3000 while training fell 2.43. In P2 validation descended 2.887, through the P1 wall and through the 
uniform-token baseline P1 never beat. Routing removed the wall; what followed is a floor, which is a different object from a value never left.

### for the generation

`checkpoint-800`: generate, then check outputs against the training text for verbatim spans. Given a P1 that produced base-vocabulary noise without a single Wake token, the prior here is the least favourable in the lineup, which is exactly what makes 
it the decisive end of the 2x2.
