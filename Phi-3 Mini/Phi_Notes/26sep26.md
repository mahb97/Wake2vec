Four consecutive rises, and the criterion is met without qualification.

**Training is monotone across all six intervals**: 8.165028, 8.094781, 8.033964, 7.983980, 7.930164, 7.859999, 7.822573, falling every time. **Validation rises at every interval from 800 onward**: +0.0176, +0.0138, +0.0229, +0.0078. **The gap widens at every interval without exception**: +0.182, +0.253, +0.313, +0.380, +0.448, +0.541, +0.586, more than tripling across 600 steps.

Phi is the only model in the lineup to satisfy the divergence criterion cleanly. TinyLlama and the small Llamas turned on softer evidence; the Llama 8B and Mistral turned but were read against noisier curves; Qwen cannot satisfy it at all, its training curve reversing eight times in thirteen intervals.

**The minimum is unusually broad rather than sharp.** The validation decrement series reads -1.0, -1.423, -1.127, -0.270, -0.053, -0.014, +0.0004, -0.0007, then the four rises. The curve decelerated smoothly through zero and lingered there for 200 steps within a range of 0.000693 before accelerating away. That is what produced the asymptote reading held between the 6th and the 17th: not a wall, but a minimum flat enough to be mistaken for one at three-decimal resolution.

The two phases must not be conflated. In P1 validation never descended: a shallow minimum at step 300 (12.233), then a rise to 12.288 by step 3000 while training fell 2.43. In P2 validation descended 2.887, through the P1 wall and through the 
uniform-token baseline P1 never beat. Routing removed the wall; what followed is a floor, which is a different object from a value never left.
