### for step 600

One question remains available to this phase: whether 8.347 is a fixed value or a waypoint. A third evaluation within roughly 0.001 would place Phi in the same reporting category as the Llama 3.2-3B, a value quotable to three decimals that the run does 
not pass, and would close the language-modelling side entirely. It also resolves the turn question by implication. A model whose validation has not moved across 200 steps while its learning rate sits near peak will not turn later under a decaying schedule. Phi would then join Qwen as the second model without a 
confirmed turn, for the opposite reason: Qwen's is unresolvable because its training curve reverses direction every second evaluation, whereas Phi's would simply not have occurred.

The floor is confirmed on a decrement series of -1.0, -1.423, -1.127, -0.270, -0.053, +0.0004, and confirmed at approximately 97% of peak learning rate, so it reflects a constraint met by the model rather than a schedule annealing out. In P1 validation 
never descended: it found a shallow minimum at step 300 (12.233) and then rose to 12.288 by step 3000, a movement of +0.055 in the wrong direction across 2,700 steps, while training loss fell 2.43. In P2 validation descended 2.887, through the P1 wall 
and through the uniform-token baseline P1 never beat, before flattening. Routing removed the P1 wall; what P2 subsequently found is a floor, which is a different object from a value never left.

The 2x2 therefore rests on the floor comparison. Against its controlled partner Mistral, matched in vocabulary, Wake share, initialisation, family, cadence and hyperparameters and differing only in pretraining data, Phi floors 3.64 above the 4.705 
minimum. Both models improved substantially under routing, so the pretraining-data effect appears as a higher achievable floor. The morphology is an asymptotic approach to that floor, distinct from the 3B's capacity wall in the approach rather than 
the destination: the 3B reached its fixed value within 100 steps and never moved again, while Phi took 600 steps of steadily collapsing decrements to arrive at one. P2's LoRA trains on Finnegans Wake text, so validation can fall on register, 
punctuation density and common-token distribution with no improvement at Wake-neologism positions. The floor height is a fact about fit.
