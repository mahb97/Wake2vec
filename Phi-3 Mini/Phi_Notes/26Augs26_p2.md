300-to-400 interval is the first one the run has spent at peak LR (cosine has decayed roughly 0.3% by step 400, so effectively the maximum of the entire run). the descent slowed by a factor of four exactly where the schedule was most favourable 
to it continuing. a decrement collapse late in a cosine run is the schedule annealing out; a decrement collapse at the LR maximum is the model meeting something. 

three shape-calls have been refuted in this project in the last week (the adapter-transient decay, the 8B's decelerating limb, Mistral's linear limb, the last of them at the final point of a six-point curve). so one eval does not establish a floor 
and 500 is the confirm. but this point is not the same kind of evidence as those were. the earlier Phi decrements scattered inside a 1.0 to 1.423 band, and -0.270 is not a scatter inside that band, it is a quarter of its lower edge. the previous 
refutations all involved calling a trend from values that stayed within their own spread; this value left the spread. so: not a floor yet, and a much stronger single point than anything the refuted calls were built on.

for step 500: a second small decrement (roughly -0.1 to -0.3, val landing near 8.1 to 8.3) and the floor is real and the run is in it. a return to -0.8 or steeper and 400 was a slow point on a descent that continues, and the floor question reopens at 600.

## cohort

the only legitimate comparator is Mistral: 32K vocab, 58% Wake share, spherical 1.5x init, same family, differing only in pretraining data, and now finished at best val 4.705. a Phi floor around 8.3 sits roughly 3.6 above it under an identical protocol.
that would mean the data wall did not disappear at P2, it moved. not a wall against descent (Phi descended 3.8 and cleared its own baseline) but a higher floor: the textbook prior costing a fixed amount of achievable fit that routing recovers only part 
of. that is the stronger and more publishable half of the 2x2, because it survives into the phase that was supposed to rescue it.

the alternative remains live: 2,600 steps of cosine runway remain, and a slow grind from 8.4 into the 6s or 5s over the next thousand steps would put Phi in Mistral's neighbourhood and make the data-wall effect phase-localised, real in P1 and 
survivable by P2. 

val can fall on register, punctuation density, and common-token distribution with no improvement at Wake-neologism positions at all. the floor height is a fact about fit, not about transformation, and even a Phi that ground down to Mistral's range 
would leave the transformation question untouched.
